SOLVED: what the traffic steps should have been asserting
======================================================================
pc-3099 (10.3.99.1), 8.7.0 LAB 935. Chassis 10.1.70.108, app server
10.1.90.108, IxNetwork 9.00. 2026-09-09.

Once the statistics view existed at all
(evidence_traffic_item_tracking.txt), the view returned numbers, and the
numbers said the expectations were wrong. Three independent chassis
runs agreed to the frame:

  TI_AC1_TO_AC2   Tx 1000   Rx 2000
  TI_AC2_TO_AC1   Tx 1000   Rx 1000    Loss 0
  TI_AC3_TO_AC1   Tx 1000   Rx 1000    Loss 0

DEFECT 1 - every step asserted "everything is running"
  The emitter computed the step's own expectation and then discarded it,
  emitting ALL_TRAFFIC_ITEMS_RUNNING for all four traffic steps. At most
  of those points exactly ONE item is unsuspended, so the assertion
  could not pass on a device behaving correctly:

    Fail: Expected:(TX_FRAME_RATE:1000), DUT output:(TX_FRAME_RATE:[0]),
          Condition:(TRAFFIC_ITEM: TI_AC2_TO_AC1)

  Each VERIFY_IXIA step now carries its own `expect_rows`, emitted as
  its own RowDataTable[]. A step with no rows is emitted EMPTY, which
  EvpnUtils reports as a warning rather than a pass - the fake-pass rule
  applied to traffic exactly as it is applied to show output.

DEFECT 2 - expected Rx is not expected Tx
  AC2 and AC3 are two sub-interfaces of ONE vport, told apart only by
  their VLAN tag (Exaware, 2026-09-08: "An AC can reside as a tagged
  interface"). A traffic item's Rx counter belongs to its destination
  VPORT. The frames are broadcast - this build reports "Unknown MAC
  Flooding: Disabled" and offers no CLI to enable it, so an unknown
  unicast is dropped before the bridge domain - so the EVI floods each
  frame to both circuits and the destination port counts it twice.

  Rx = 2x Tx is therefore not an anomaly to be tolerated. It IS the
  flooding assertion: if the EVI stopped flooding to one circuit it
  reads 1000 and the step fails. The multiple is now derived from the
  lab profile, so a rig with one circuit per port gets 1x with no edit.

WHAT THE RIG CANNOT PROVE, AND WHERE THE PROOF ACTUALLY IS
  Three step titles claimed things these counters cannot show, and
  under the project's own rule a step title may not apologise for the
  rig. They were rewritten to state what they genuinely establish:

  - "flooding to AC3 ceases once AC2's MACs are known" - impossible.
    The frames are broadcast and broadcast is always flooded. AC1's rx
    stayed at 2000 through every later step, exactly as it should.
  - "AC1 -> AC2 still forwards to AC2 (no flooding)" - same reason.
  - "AC1 -> AC2 now forwards out AC3" - invisible here: AC2 and AC3
    share a vport, so traffic leaving by either lands in the same rx
    bucket.

  Egress tracking was tested as a way to split that bucket by VLAN. The
  chassis refused the encapsulation ("The list of available offsets is:
  Custom, CustomByField, CustomByFieldAutoValues") and a Custom bit
  offset at the VLAN field did not separate the counters either. Flow
  Statistics breaks down by Tx/Rx PORT, which does not help when both
  circuits are on one port.

  The MAC move is asserted where the evidence really is, on the DUT,
  and those steps pass today:
    FLOW-030.S15  show evpn mac-address-table ... source  (MACs on AC3)
    FLOW-030.S16  no new Type-2 triggered vs the snapshot

DEFECT 3 - TC03 aged out MACs it had never learnt
  FLOW-031 went straight to "stop the traffic, wait out the aging time,
  assert the MACs are gone" from a prep that starts every item
  SUSPENDED. Nothing was ever learnt, so the aged-out check, the
  withdrawn-route check and the flooding check were all trivially true
  on an empty MAC table. The flow now starts AC1 and AC3 traffic and
  asserts the MACs ARE learnt before it tests that they age out.

TWO WAYS A CAPTURED ABSENCE EXPECTATION LIES, BOTH FIXED
  `verifyShowLinesAbsent` demands its lines be GONE, so what goes into
  one matters more than for a presence check.

  1. Table furniture. The MAC-table capture carried `IP` and
     `VLAN MAC ADDRESS LOC SOURCE ...` next to the MAC row. Column
     headers are printed whether or not a single entry remains, so
     "verify the MACs aged out" demanded that the words MAC ADDRESS
     disappear - it could never pass. `fake_pass.is_structural` existed
     for precisely this and the emitter never called it.

  2. Rows that are still present, correctly. FLOW-031 ages out ONE
     circuit while AC1 keeps transmitting, so the capture of the
     unscoped `show bgp l2vpn evpn table evi detail` also held AC1's
     Type-2 route. Asserting its withdrawal fails against a healthy
     device. Absence steps on an unscoped command now carry an
     `expect_subject` and keep only the lines naming it:

       FLOW031_S05_TYPE2_WITHDRAWN_LINES
         before: Type=2: ... MAC=00:00:01:00:00:01   (AC1 - never withdrawn)
                 ESI=0:0000.0000.0000.0000.0000
                 ... 
         after : Type=2: ... MAC=00:00:02:00:00:01   (the aged MAC, only)
