2. THREE DOCUMENT-DERIVED DECISIONS THE DEVICE OVERTURNED
----------------------------------------------------------
   a) `mac address-table` (space) vs `mac-address-table` (hyphen).
      We had resolved this in favour of the SPACE, reasoning from three
      agreeing sources: the `clear` syntax in the same CLI doc, the VPLS family
      in the Command Reference Guide, and Exaware's own production `Commands`
      enum. The device rejects the space form and lists the hyphen form.
      **The CLI-doc syntax cell we overrode was right.** Corrected.

      Keep this as the standing example: device output outranks any number of
      agreeing documents.

   b) `show evpn global` does not exist. The real commands are
      `show evpn summary` and `show evpn detail`. Corrected.

   c) `import-rt` / `export-rt` are NOT direct children of the EVI.
      `l2-services evpn <name> ?` offers only auto-discovery, interface,
      mac-aging-time, mac-limit, service-type; the route targets live one level
      down under `auto-discovery`. Corrected.
