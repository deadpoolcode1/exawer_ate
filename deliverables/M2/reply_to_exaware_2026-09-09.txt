Running it found four defects of ours, all fixed:

- Our traffic items carried no tracking, so IxNetwork never built the
  "Traffic Item Statistics" view and every traffic assertion failed
  while the traffic was running correctly. ixia_lib's trafficApply
  reads that view on its last line, which is where the misleading
  "ERROR-7008 Could not apply traffic" came from.
- Expected Rx assumed Rx = Tx. AC2 and AC3 share a vport, so a flooded
  frame is counted twice: TI_AC1_TO_AC2 is Tx 1000 / Rx 2000. That 2x
  is now the flooding assertion.
- TC03 aged out MACs it had never learnt, because its prep starts every
  traffic item suspended. It learns first now.
- Our absence checks asked once. Your MAC aging time is 300 s but the
  sweep is coarser: measured here, the entry was still present at 342 s
  and gone by 442 s. They poll now.

Expectations were re-captured against a device with traffic running:
16 of 16 usable. Falsifiable assertions went from 7 of 26 to 17 of 26.

Two on your side:

- bgpd aborts when an EVI is deleted: assert (_Bool)(ipi_evi_p),
  bgp_evi.c:310, bgp_evi_delete. The EVI then survives in operational
  state after leaving the configuration, so nothing starts clean
  without a reboot. Backtrace in 02_evidence/.
- Bring-up runs "less /var/log/syslog | grep ... -c" on the serial
  console. less opens /dev/tty and waits for a keypress, so it times
  out at 100 s. Not a size problem: it hung on a 7.9 KB file. cat in
  place of less fixes it.
  Separately, bring-up matches the ONL shell on the literal
  "@localhost", and the hostname goes back to "router" a few minutes
  after boot, so any suite can lose that race.
