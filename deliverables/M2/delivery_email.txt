What was actually blocking TC02

Not the testbed, and nothing on your side. Three of these are worth your
attention because they are properties of the product or the framework rather
than of our generator:

1. A vlan-based attachment circuit needs an explicit `vlan-id`. Configuring
   `interface x-eth 0/0/18.3380` with only `l2-transport enable` gives a
   circuit that is admin-up, is listed by `show evpn detail` as bound, and
   classifies nothing: the port counted 219,000 frames received while the
   sub-interface counted 0. The sub-interface number does not select the VLAN.

2. `show evpn mac-address-table name <evi> source x-eth 0/0/8.3380` is
   rejected with "syntax error: unknown argument", while every configuration
   command accepts the same interface written with the space. The show filter
   wants the spelling the table prints (`x-eth0/0/8.3380`). If that asymmetry
   is not intentional it may be worth a look.

3. The IXIA source MAC was settable all along. The field is
   ethernet.header.sourceAddress-2, not -1 — the suffix is the field's
   position in the ethernet stack, not part of its name. This removes the
   dependency we had previously reported on a source-MAC proc being added to
   ixia_lib.tcl. No change to your infrastructure file is needed.


The underlay

Ilan's point that the configuration was missing BGP and IGP was correct, and
it is fixed. The generated EVPN config previously stated in its own header
that the underlay had to arrive from cleanBaseConfig, and it never did — a
clean base configures no IGP and no BGP, so EVPN could not come up as an
overlay at all. That is also why the `show bgp l2vpn evpn table` expectations
were always empty: there was no BGP session, rather than no peer.

The generator now emits OSPF, LDP and BGP with `af-l2vpn evpn`, modelled on
your VPLS_N1.cfg, and it commits on the device. Per your note we assert EVPN
in the session capabilities rather than route exchange:

    L2VPN EVPN:     advertised

Verified both ways — removing `af-l2vpn evpn` from the generated config makes
the device answer `L2VPN EVPN: none` and turns the test red.

Also per your note, the IXIA now runs OSPF and LDP as well as BGP, and OSPF
reaches FULL with the DUT. That was a real gap: we were configuring the
device to speak three protocols into a port configured for none of them.


Two limits, stated plainly

- No Type-2/Type-3 route exchange has been demonstrated. Emulating a BGP EVPN
  speaker on the IXIA fails with "ERROR-1005 ... There is no license available
  for BGP EVPN" on chassis 10.1.70.108. It is specific to that feature — the
  same neighbour starts and establishes with plain ipv4-unicast. Not needed
  for the current TCs, per your guidance; it would be needed to assert
  advertisement and withdrawal against a real peer.

- pc-3080 has exactly three DUT<->IXIA links. Using one as the L3 core leaves
  two attachment circuits, so the three-AC MAC-move flow and the core link
  cannot both run in one configuration. Both configurations are delivered and
  both are green; they are separate runs. A fourth link would remove the
  choice.
