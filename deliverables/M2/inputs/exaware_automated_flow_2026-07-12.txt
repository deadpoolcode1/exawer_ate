Per my comment from Thursday, these are the flows we can address

  * Flow-010, Vlan-based EVPN bringup.
  * Flow-030, Flow-031 – Type-2 and Type-3 advertisement.
  * Both use Ixia for local traffic.

Required modifications to the TP to support a single DuT+ 3 Ixia ports :

  * Flow-010 is a pre-requisite for Flows 030 and 031.
  * Flow 030/031
     * Configuring 3 local ACs.

Detailed Automated flow will be:

  * 3 Ixia traffic items, AC2 and AC3 having the same MAC addresses.
     * AC1 to AC2
     * AC2 to AC1
     * AC3 to AC1
  * Establish a service with 3 ACs and EVPN neighbouring PE
  * Verify relevant shows
     * Show evpn global, show evpn mac-address, show bgp l2vpn evpn...
     * Verify type-3 is reported as advertised
  * Send traffic from AC1 to AC2
     * Verify traffic flooding to AC2 and AC3 (Using Dut counters and Ixia counters)
     * Verify AC1 src MACs learnt from correct interface
     * Verify AC1 x src Type-2 are advertised
  * Send traffic from AC2 to AC1
     * Verify flooding ceases to AC3
     * Verify AC1 src MACs are in tact
     * Verify AC2 src MACs learnt from correct interface
     * Verify AC2 src MACs Type-2 are also advertised
  * Stop AC2 to AC1 traffic
     * Verify AC1 to AC2 traffic still flows to AC2
  * Start traffic from AC3 to AC1
     * Verify AC2 MAC addresses shifted to AC3
     * Verify no type-2 message was triggered
     * Verify AC1 to AC2 traffic flows to AC3.
  * Stop traffic AC3 to AC1
     * Wait for aging time to expire
     * AC1 to AC2 traffic floods to AC2 and AC3
     * AC2 MACs are removed from the evpn mac-table
     * AC2 MACs type-2 msgs are withdrawn from the BGP table.
