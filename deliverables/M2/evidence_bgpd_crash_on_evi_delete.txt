DEFECT: bgpd aborts when an EVPN instance is deleted
======================================================================

WHAT

  The commit reports "Commit complete"; bgpd dies and respawns.

  2. l2-services evpn evi-1 service-type vlan-based, bind a
     sub-interface, commit
