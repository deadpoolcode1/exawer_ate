bgpd aborts when an EVPN instance is deleted while BGP is established
======================================================================

severity: process abort (SIGABRT) on the control-plane daemon

WHAT HAPPENS
  With a BGP session established to the tester and the L2VPN EVPN
  address family configured on the neighbour, removing the EVPN
  instance aborts bgpd on a failed assertion. The commit itself
  reports "Commit complete"; bgpd dies and is respawned.

WHEN IT HAPPENS, STATED PRECISELY
  Confirmed by direct reproduction with the BGP session ESTABLISHED (see
  below). Four bgpd cores were produced on this box today, at 11:28:12,
  12:35:45, 13:20:06 and 13:25:55. The first two predate the point at which
  the session first reached Established (about 13:20), so the abort is NOT
  conditional on an established session: a configured neighbour carrying the
  L2VPN EVPN address family appears to be enough. We have not isolated which
  of the two is the trigger, and we are not claiming to have.

  What is certain: every one of those four cores is the same assertion in
  the same function, and each coincides with an EVPN instance being removed.

MINIMAL REPRODUCTION
  1. bring up the underlay so the BGP neighbour reaches Established
     with af-l2vpn evpn configured;
  2. configure  l2-services evpn evi-1 service-type vlan-based  with
     one or more sub-interfaces bound, and commit;
  3. no l2-services evpn evi-1  and commit.
  bgpd aborts. Observed as a PID change across step 3:
     before: pid 30551, started 13:26
     after : pid 32180, started 13:31
